Our Story

Built on Trust & Excellence

Career Serve Limited — commonly referred to as "Career Serve" — is a premier HR outsourcing firm established in 2015 under the Companies Act in Kenya. With a robust track record of growth and innovation, the company has established a significant footprint in both Kenyan and global markets, specializing in tailored HR services including Talent Acquisition, Customer Support, Payroll Management, Outsourced HR Management, and more.

"Career Serve is renowned for its expert team and bespoke solutions that cater to the unique needs of each client, distinguishing itself in a competitive industry by its commitment to excellence and integrity."

Founded to meet the growing needs for global specialized HR services, Career Serve has since expanded its reach — forming strategic alliances with key stakeholders across various industries in both the public and private sectors.
