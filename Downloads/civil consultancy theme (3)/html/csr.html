Imani Christian School, Embu County

A cornerstone of our CSR initiatives is our ongoing support for Imani Christian School in Embu County, Kenya — a fully sponsored institution dedicated to providing quality education to orphaned and needy children, many of whom have been deeply affected by the HIV/AIDS epidemic.

Established in 2017, Imani Christian School represents everything Career Serve believes about investing in people. By supporting the school, we not only contribute to the education of underprivileged children but also invest in the future of the community by nurturing the next generation of leaders.

Beyond Financial Contributions

Our involvement extends beyond funding. Career Serve staff regularly engage in volunteer activities and capacity-building programs to actively support the school's growth, development, and long-term sustainability.

We believe that a business that uplifts its community creates stronger foundations for everyone — and Imani Christian School stands as a symbol of that belief in action.
