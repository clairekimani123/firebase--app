Service Policies

Which industries do you specialize in?

We primarily focus on industrial infrastructure, high-rise commercial and residential towers, public transport civil works (bridges, tunnels, railways), and renewable energy facilities including solar farms and hydroelectric plants.

Are your consultations available internationally?

Yes, while our headquarters is in Nairobi, Kenya, we provide remote design consulting and on-site project management for global infrastructure projects across Africa, the Middle East, and Southeast Asia.

What sustainability certifications do you support?

We assist clients in achieving LEED, BREEAM, WELL, and EDGE certifications through optimized structural design, sustainable material selection, and energy-efficient building practices.

What is your pricing structure?

We offer flexible engagement models including fixed-fee for defined scopes, time-and-materials for ongoing advisory, and value-based pricing for large-scale infrastructure projects. Contact us for a customized quote.
